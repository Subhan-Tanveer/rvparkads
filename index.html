Seller FAQs

Frequently Asked Seller Questions

Everything you need to know about listing timelines, contract flexibility, and cancellation policies.

01How long does it take for my RV park listing to go live?+

Most listings go live within one business day of completing your park details after checkout.

02What contract terms or minimum commitments are required?+

Sell It Yourself is $99/month and Sell With Marie is $49/month, both with a 3-month minimum. After that you can cancel any month.

03What is your cancellation policy?+

Cancel anytime after your minimum term (if any) — just contact us and we'll take your listing down.

04How are buyer inquiries handled and delivered?+

Inquiries are forwarded straight to the email and phone number you provide when you complete your listing.

05Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan after getting started?+

Yes — reach out any time and we'll adjust your plan for the next billing cycle.

06What’s the difference between advertising here and selling with a broker?+

Sell It Yourself ($99/month): we market your park, there’s no commission, and buyers contact you directly. Sell With Marie ($49/month): Marie Hansen of RVParkSales is your listing broker and handles buyers, negotiation and closing; she is paid a commission only if your park sells, as set in your listing agreement.

07Can you help connect me with buyer financing resources?+

Yes — we can introduce serious buyers to experienced RV park lenders to help move a strong inquiry toward a close.
